# Excel for Data Analysis

Learn Excel from the ground up with **Past Hundred**. This module of the **Full-Stack Data Analysis course** takes you from your first Excel lesson through formulas, PivotTables, a dashboard project, Power Query, and working with ChatGPT in Excel.

**Course videos and files:** [Open the Excel course on Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1QmlP1Iw6pJvQ5xwXkcUgbI6gswXVi9bH?usp=sharing)

## How I teach

I introduce the Excel term, explain it in plain language, and use an everyday analogy when it helps. If a new idea depends on something earlier, I go back and explain that first. Then I show the idea in Excel and give you an exercise to try on your own.

Each topic has practice material. The course also includes **12 numbered exercise sessions**, each with a separate solution workbook. The datasets are for practice. Change a few inputs as you follow along and see whether your results match what you expected.

## Start here

1. Open the course on Google Drive and start with **000 Excel Introduction for Beginners**.
2. Open **0000 Excel Class Files** in Drive to get the same class materials used in the lessons.
3. For each topic, follow along in the matching practice file. Then close the lesson and try the exercise on your own.
4. Compare your work with the matching solution workbook. Check the formulas and the result, then revisit the lesson if you get stuck.
5. Use **Exercise 1–9 (video)** in Drive for the recorded exercise walkthroughs. When you reach the dashboard project, use Lesson 013 to document and share your work.

## Exercises and solutions

The exercise workbooks give you a chance to apply what you learned before opening the worked solution. Keep the exercise and solution filenames as shown so you can match them by session number.

| Sessions | Exercise workbooks | Solution workbooks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–11 | `Session1_Exercise.xlsx` through `Session11_Exercise.xlsx` | `Session1_Solution.xlsx` through `Session11_Solution.xlsx` |
| 12 | `Session12_Exercise.xlsx` | `Session12_Solution.xlsm` |

Session 12’s solution is a macro-enabled workbook. Keep the `.xlsm` extension when saving or sharing it.

## Dashboard project

The dashboard lesson starts with the ideas behind a dashboard and takes you through building one. Use the project to practice explaining what the data shows. When you share your version, include a screenshot and a short description of the question you answered, what you built, and what you found.
